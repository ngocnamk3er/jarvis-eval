Travel wishlist — someday

- Rottnest Island, Australia: quokkas, the small marsupials that always
  look like they're smiling. You can't feed them or pick them up though.
- Ha Long Bay, Vietnam: limestone karsts, quiet kayaking at dawn.
- Hokkaido, Japan: snow festival in February, lavender fields in July.
- Faroe Islands: grass-roof houses, a lot of sheep, dramatic cliffs.
