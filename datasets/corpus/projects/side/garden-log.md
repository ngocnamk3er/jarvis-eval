# Balcony garden log

## Basil
Repotted 2026-08. Loves the morning sun spot. Pinch the flower buds to
keep it leafy.

## Chilli (bird's eye)
Slow start, picked up after moving next to the basil. First fruit set late
August. Water when the top inch is dry, not before.

## Mint
In its own pot on purpose — it takes over anything it shares soil with.
