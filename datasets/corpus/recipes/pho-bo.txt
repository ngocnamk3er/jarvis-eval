Phở bò — quick weeknight version

Broth: char an onion and a thumb of ginger over the flame. Simmer with
beef bones, a cinnamon stick, 3 star anise, 2 cloves, a black cardamom pod
and a pinch of coriander seed for at least 1.5 hours. Season with fish
sauce and a little rock sugar.

Serve: soft rice noodles in the bowl, thin raw beef slices on top, ladle
the boiling broth over. Plate of Thai basil, bean sprouts, lime, chilli on
the side.
