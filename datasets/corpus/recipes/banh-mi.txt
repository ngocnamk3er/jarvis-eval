Bánh mì — assembly notes

Baguette warmed until the crust cracks. Inside: a thin layer of pâté,
Kewpie mayo, cold cuts or grilled pork, pickled carrot + daikon (đồ chua),
cucumber batons, coriander, a few slices of chilli, a dash of Maggi.

The đồ chua is the whole thing: julienne carrot + daikon, 1:1 sugar and
rice vinegar, a little salt, rest 1 hour minimum.
